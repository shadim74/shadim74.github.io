The training run

Machine learning engineer in Toronto. My career, logged the way I'd log a model: every move was a warm restart, where the loss jumps and then settles lower than before.

2013

Initialization

B.Sc. Electrical Engineering

    1. Initialization2013 — 2017

      B.Sc. Electrical Engineering

      Amirkabir University of Technology, Tehran. Electronics stream.

      Circuits, signals and instrumentation. The thesis was a PC-based oscilloscope: hardware for looking closely at signals, which turned out to be the job ever since.

    2. PretrainingJun 2018 — Aug 2019

      Research Software Engineer

      IPM, Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences, Tehran. Internship.

      First machine learning on real signals: ML and DSP models for emotional state recognition from EEG, with spatial feature extraction and cross-subject generalization evaluation. Models that had to work on people they never saw in training.

    3. Warm restartSep 2019 — 2022

      M.Sc. Electrical & Computer Engineering

      Ontario Tech University, Oshawa. Also Research & Teaching Assistant.

      A new country and a harder objective. Research on pre-coder design for 5G large antenna systems, with convergence and computational-complexity ablations against published baselines, reaching substantially lower complexity at equivalent performance. The thesis on fast joint unicast and multicast beamforming became a first-author paper in IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing.

    4. Validation2021

      Best Student Paper Award, IEEE SPAWC

      Lucca, Italy

      Awarded for the conference version of the thesis work: a fast algorithm for joint unicast and multicast beamforming in large-scale systems.

    5. Domain shiftJan 2022 — Aug 2024

      Graduate Technology Program (GTLP)

      TELUS Communications, Toronto. Rotational graduate program.

      Into industry through two rotations: first software, then machine learning. Alongside them, I was the prime for the GTLP AI Learning Community for a year, running workshops and launching four applied AI initiatives.

      Jan — Sep 2022
      Software Developer, Home Network Dev team. Automated hardware and lab testing with Python and Groovy in Jenkins, and built a live test-results dashboard in Dash, React and Flask over PostgreSQL to triage regressions.
      Oct 2022 — Aug 2024
      Machine Learning Engineer, AI Accelerator team. Production ML over large-scale network telemetry, the start of the work in the next checkpoint.
    6. ProductionAug 2024 — present

      Machine Learning Engineer

      TELUS Communications, AI Accelerator team, Toronto

      Stayed on the AI Accelerator team after the program, prototyping, evaluating and productionizing ML systems end to end: LLM, RAG and agentic applications alongside classic predictive models over large-scale network telemetry.

      Much of the work is evaluation: turning business goals into success metrics, golden datasets and LLM-as-a-judge setups, then shipping reproducible, monitored pipelines on GCP.

    7. Still training2026

      The loss is still going down

      Always glad to talk about data-centric ML, anomaly detection, evaluation, and getting models to behave in production.
